KC High Level Training Plan

One locked run-volume path from 1 September to Border 50K on 19 December: 300 km in September, a 100 km peak week ending 1 November, outside-NCR long runs during the pollution months, and a controlled taper into race day.

Locked Milestones

September: 300 km

Five 10K runs and one 20K long run per full week. The final partial September block contributes 20 km so the month closes at exactly 300.

Peak: 100 km

The Oct 26-Nov 1 week is the only planned 100 km week, with a 34 km outside-NCR long run.

Border: 19 Dec

The final 30 km specific long run lands by 6 Dec, followed by a 55 km taper week and race week.

Execution Rules

Wednesday Default

Wednesday remains the normal long-run day. Start early enough to finish, refuel and be home before 8:00 AM.

Pollution Rule

From October onward, do not force long runs in NCR pollution. Plan the route and travel outside NCR in advance.

Recovery Gate

The targets are locked, but pain, altered gait or recovery beyond 48 hours still overrides a single workout. Do not compensate by stacking missed distance.
