KC High Level Training Plan

The complete three-option weekly running-volume model through Border 50K. The run-first phase ends at Border; after recovery, the main focus moves to triathlon training while the run base is maintained for TMM.

Jaisalmer 50K Running Volume Options

The original model anchored on 17.5 km for 22-28 June and a projected 30 km for 29 June-5 July. Plan A stabilizes near 60 km, Plan B builds toward 80 km with holds and cutbacks, and Plan C is the aggressive high-risk wave option.

Plan A: Hold 60

Use when durability, back/hip tolerance, or life stress is the constraint. It reaches 60 later and treats that range as the main ceiling.

Plan B: Build To 80

The original default if early weeks were absorbed well. It uses holds and cutbacks before anchoring higher volume.

Plan C: Wave To 100

Aggressive option only with consistently clean recovery. It carries the highest injury cost and should not be selected while the back is uncertain.
