Border 50K Pacing Plan

Execution plan for the planned Border 50K, Jaisalmer qualifier. The target is not to show speed; it is to bank an official sub-6 50K while keeping enough recovery for the January marathon build.

Race date in plan: 19 Dec 2026 KC floor: under 6:00 Operating target: 5:55 Average for 6:00: 7:12/km

Race Read

Course Shape

The local route file measures about 50.18 km, with roughly 392 m gain and 455 m loss. Elevation ranges from about 161 m to 267 m.

Terrain Risk

Plan for low-altitude desert running with wind, exposed road or mixed surfaces, dry air, sand/gravel patches, and support gaps. Pace by effort first.

Admin Gate

Use this as the KC 50K only after confirming official timing, result publication, certificate quality, and Ladakh Marathon acceptance.

Target Strategy

Primary Target

5:55 finish gives about 5 minutes of buffer under the KC requirement. That is roughly 7:05/km over 50.1 km, but the watch pace should not be chased blindly on loose surface or into wind.

Effort Rule

First 30 km should feel like an easy long run with discipline. From 30-40 km, hold form. Only from 40 km should the plan become pace-protective.

Fuel And Fluids

Calories

  • Start fueling by 25-30 minutes.
  • Target 45-60 g carbohydrate per hour if gut training supports it.
  • Use the same gels/drink mix rehearsed in long runs. No race-day experiments.

Hydration

  • Drink to schedule, not only thirst.
  • Use sodium consistently; dry desert air can hide sweat loss.
  • If aid is sparse, carry enough fluid to bridge the longest unsupported section.

Decision Points

Point Green Yellow Red
20 km Breathing easy, stomach calm, legs normal. Minor heat/wind cost. Keep effort, ignore pace. Back/hip changes gait. Switch to finish-safe mode.
30 km Can still speak in short phrases and fuel normally. Heavy legs but stable form. Add walk breaks only at aid. Nausea, cramps, or gait change. Protect health over target.
40 km Sub-6 buffer intact. Lock rhythm and finish. Buffer thin. Run flats/downs, hike only rough patches. Target gone. Finish only if safe and useful.

Training Checks

  • By late October: 70-80 km weeks should be manageable without back flare-ups.
  • By November: complete at least one 32-36 km long run with fueling every 30-35 minutes.
  • Before taper: rehearse the exact race shoe, socks, bottle/vest setup, gels, sodium, and breakfast.
  • If long-run recovery exceeds 48 hours repeatedly, downgrade the race target or use a backup qualifier.
