Border 50K Pacing Plan

Detailed execution plan for the planned Border 50K, Jaisalmer qualifier.

Race date in plan: 19 Dec 2026 KC floor: under 6:00 Working target: ~5:50 elapsed Average for 6:00: 7:12/km

Race Read

Course Shape

The GPX measures about 50.99 km. It starts around 246 m, drops sharply to about 172 m by 10 km, climbs to about 217 m by 30 km, and finishes near 185 m. The sparse GPX points suggest roughly 132 m gain and 195 m loss; treat those totals as approximate.

Terrain Risk

Plan for low-altitude desert running with wind, exposed road or mixed surfaces, dry air, sand/gravel patches, and support gaps. Pace by effort first.

Target Strategy

Primary Target

Run around 6:45/km moving pace over the 50.99 km GPX course. That gives roughly 5:44 moving time; reserve up to 6 minutes for water and aid stops, producing a worst-case plan of about 5:50 elapsed.

Effort Rule

Use the early descent for a modest time advantage, but do not race it. Expect the 20-30 km climb to be slower by effort. Keep water stops short and deliberate; the six-minute stop budget is part of the race plan, not extra time.

Split Plan

40-50 km Rolling with a net descent toward the finish, near 185 m. ~1:11 moving ~5:44 moving Hold roughly 6:45/km moving pace. Press only if the legs, gut, and heat response are stable.

Fuel And Fluids

Calories

  • Start fueling by 25-30 minutes.
  • Target 45-60 g carbohydrate per hour if gut training supports it.
  • Use the same gels/drink mix rehearsed in long runs. No race-day experiments.

Hydration

  • Drink to schedule, not only thirst.
  • Use sodium consistently; dry desert air can hide sweat loss.
  • If aid is sparse, carry enough fluid to bridge the longest unsupported section.
  • Budget no more than 6 total minutes stopped at water/aid points; the 5:50 plan includes these stops.

Training Checks

  • By late October: the September 70 km baseline should feel stable, and the 100 km peak week should be attempted only with no back flare-up or gait change.
  • By November: complete at least one 32-36 km long run with fueling every 30-35 minutes.
  • Before taper: rehearse the exact race shoe, socks, bottle/vest setup, gels, sodium, and breakfast.
  • If long-run recovery exceeds 48 hours repeatedly, downgrade the race target and protect consistent training.
