Run Form Fix Plan

Summary and correction plan from the 4 Jul 2026 treadmill form review. The goal is not cosmetic form change; it is lower braking, better frontal-plane stability, and enough tissue tolerance for the Border 50K build.

Report date: 4 Jul 2026 Overall form: 4/10 Stability: 2/10 Injury risk: high

Main Findings

Overstriding

Pronounced heel strike with the foot landing ahead of the body and the knee too straight at contact. This creates braking force and higher knee load.

Overpronation

The foot lands laterally then collapses inward during loading. This leaks energy and raises shin, plantar fascia, and knee-tracking risk.

Narrow Step Width

The stance is very narrow, almost crossing the midline. This increases pronation, knee valgus, and IT-band stress.

Priority Fixes

Priority Problem Cue Drill Strength Support
1 Overstriding and braking Shorter, quicker steps. Land under hips. A-skips, high knees, short relaxed strides. Pogo jumps, calf stiffness work.
2 Tightrope / crossover stride Run on either side of a painted line. Straddling-the-line jogs and strides. Banded lateral walks, monster walks.
3 Straight knee at impact Soft knees. Gentle downhill strides only when fresh. Step-downs, eccentric single-leg squats.
4 Ankle / arch collapse Push off the big toe. Barefoot grass walking, foot-control drills. Standing and seated calf raises, towel scrunches.
5 Lateral hip control Keep knee tracking over foot. Single-leg balance and controlled bounds later. Clamshells, Bulgarian split squats, split squats.

4-Week Fix Plan

Every Easy Run

  • Use a metronome 5% above natural cadence, not 10% immediately.
  • Focus on quick, quiet steps for the first 10-15 minutes.
  • Keep the feet on either side of an imaginary line.
  • Stop cueing once fatigue makes form forced.

Before 3 Runs / Week

  • A-skips: 2 x 20 m.
  • Straddling-the-line jog: 2 x 50 m.
  • Optional relaxed strides: 4 x 20 seconds only when legs feel fresh.
Exercise Dose Purpose Placement
Banded lateral walks 3 x 15 steps each direction Glute medius, knee valgus control Warm-up or light strength day
Single-leg step-downs 3 x 8-10 each side Knee tracking and eccentric control Strength days, before heavy fatigue
Heavy calf raises 3 x 12-20 Calf, ankle, arch stiffness 2-3 times weekly
Dead bug / side plank 2-3 sets Trunk control without back irritation End of gym or mobility sessions

How This Fits The KC Build

  • Do not add aggressive speed work until 38-42 km weeks are repeatable and calves/shins stay quiet.
  • Use cadence and step-width cues during easy runs before using them in threshold sessions.
  • Keep long-run effort easy while form changes are being absorbed.
  • Consider stability shoes as a support tool if neutral shoes are not controlling collapse, but do not use shoes as a substitute for strength.
