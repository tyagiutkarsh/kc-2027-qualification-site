# KC Qualification Research Archive

This file preserves material removed from the current-facing KC qualification HTML on 8 August 2026. It is reference material, not the active plan. Dates, entry rules, certificates, and race availability must be verified before reuse.

## Qualification rules used

- Full marathon: official 42.195 km finish under 5:00:00.
- 50K ultra: official 50 km finish under 6:00:00.
- Qualification-floor paces: 7:06/km for the marathon and 7:12/km for the 50K.
- Evidence deadline used by the plan: before 1 March 2027.
- Stadium races, 24-hour races, virtual races, personal Garmin/Strava efforts, and ambiguous self-timed or self-supported events were treated as administrative risks.

## Archived race-entry eligibility notes

- **Tata Ultra 50K:** 2026 rules listed age 18+, a full marathon under 6:00 within 18 months for 50K entry, mandatory headlight, and an eight-hour race cutoff.
- **Tata Mumbai Marathon:** 2026 confirmation accepted qualifying timed on-ground marathon, 25K, or half-marathon certificates. The saved VDHM 2025 result of 1:59:40 cleared the cited 2026 HM thresholds, subject to 2027 rules and certificate acceptance.
- **Cognizant New Delhi Marathon:** 2026 rules listed age 18+, individual/non-transferable entries, chip timing, and certificate restrictions for tampered timing bibs. No prior-result entry cutoff was found during research.
- **SKF Goa River Marathon:** the checked pages emphasized registration, bib/chip timing, and official certificates; a six-hour marathon cutoff appeared in the FAQ source.
- **Vedanta Delhi Half Marathon 2026:** official 2026 criteria were not available in the checked sources. The saved 2025 reference criteria were much slower than the recorded VDHM 1:59:40 result.
- **TCS World 10K Bengaluru:** the 2026 Open 10K accepted several timing-certificate distances. The recorded VDHM result cleared the cited HM threshold, but the race was not needed for KC.
- **Chandigarh Fast Marathon:** the checked site listed strict prior-result thresholds. The recorded 1:59:40 HM cleared the listed female threshold but not the listed male threshold.
- **AU Jaipur Marathon:** 2026 terms listed age, payment/confirmation, possible medical certificate, bib collection, chip timing, and official-route compliance; no prior-result marathon cutoff was found.
- **Jaipur Ultra:** promising listing, but organiser identity, certificate quality, route certification, and Ladakh acceptance were unresolved.
- **Border Ultra:** historical results supported the event's existence, but the exact current edition, support, timing, route, and certificate acceptance remained an explicit gate.

## Archived generic evaluation model

- Prefer low-altitude road courses over altitude, trail, sand, rock, and technical descents for the qualification attempts.
- Prefer low elevation gain: every climb costs pace and every descent adds quad damage.
- Protect recovery time between the 50K and marathon qualifiers.
- Prefer established organisers, chip timing, published results, and clear certificates.
- Treat self-timed, self-supported, ambiguous-distance, and unclear-result events as administrative risks.
